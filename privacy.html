Privacy Policy: Myth Trail: Space Arcade

Effective date: 2026-11-07

This privacy policy explains how the Android game Myth Trail: Space Arcade ("Myth Trail", "the game", package com.myth.trail) handles information. The game is made by Insep ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, write to insep9597@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Myth Trail is a single-player game that works fully offline. You do not create an account with us.
  • We do not collect, receive or store any personal information on our own servers. We do not run any servers. Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game includes two Google services that do process data: Google AdMob (ads, with Google's consent tool) and Google Play Games Services (sign-in and leaderboards). They connect to the internet and handle data under Google's own privacy policy, described below.
  • The game has no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking tools of our own, no crash reporting service, no chat and no social features.

1. Information stored on your device

The game keeps one save file in its private app storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and the game never sends it anywhere. It contains only game data:

  • your coin balance, the cosmetic items you own and have equipped (ships, trail styles, colors);
  • your best score in each game mode, and best scores still waiting to be sent to the Play Games leaderboard;
  • your daily quests and their progress, the daily gift and milestone rewards you have claimed, and the dates they refer to (from your device's clock);
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, mirrored controls) and which tutorials you have finished;
  • simple play statistics used for the game's difficulty and milestones: the length of your last two runs, and the total number of runs, defeated rivals, destroyed planets and play time.

The save file contains no name, email address, contact list, location, photos or any other personal information. The game does not back it up to the cloud.

2. Information processed by Google services

2.1 Advertising: Google AdMob

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of menu screens and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward. There are no ads during gameplay.

When the game requests an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (or, where supported, Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features),
  • your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived),
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language),
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps), and diagnostic information about ad performance.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, show personalized ads. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

The game asks Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences.

More information:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google advertising and privacy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Data the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2.2 Your consent choices

On first launch, before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in other regions where Google's consent tool applies). You can accept or refuse personalized advertising there. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

To change your choice later: open the game, go to Settings → Privacy options. This reopens the consent form wherever your consent is managed by it.

You can also control ads at the device level:

  • On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by device) to reset or delete your advertising ID or to opt out of ad personalization.
  • Manage Google ad personalization at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

2.3 Leaderboards: Google Play Games Services

The game uses Google Play Games Services for optional leaderboards (one per game mode). When the game starts, it tries to sign you in to Play Games silently, using the Play Games profile already on your device; if you are not signed in, the Leaderboard screen offers a sign-in button. You can play the whole game without signing in.

If you are signed in:

  • the game sends your best score for each mode to Google's leaderboard for that mode;
  • the game shows leaderboard pages from Google, which contain the Play Games names (gamer tags), ranks and scores of the players on the board, including yours;
  • Google processes your Play Games profile and your scores under the Google Privacy Policy, and other players may see your Play Games name and score according to your Play Games profile visibility settings.

The game does not read your email address, real name, contacts or friends list, and does not store your Play Games ID.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/
  • Manage your Google account and its data: https://myaccount.google.com/

5. Children

Myth Trail is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal information ourselves, we hold nothing about any child; if you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that caused Google services to process their data, contact us at insep9597@gmail.com and we will help you reach Google to have it removed.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Save file: kept on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Myth Trail → Storage → Clear data), deletes it permanently. We have no copy to restore it from.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time (Section 2.2).
  • Play Games data (profile, scores): kept by Google in your Google account. You can manage or delete it through the Play Games app settings or your Google account (https://myaccount.google.com/).

7. Security

Your save file lives in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. All communication with Google's ad and Play Games services is handled by Google's SDKs over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed.

8. International users and your rights

The game is distributed worldwide through Google Play. Google may process data in countries other than your own under its own safeguards.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR): personalized advertising relies on your consent, given or refused in the consent form and changeable at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Since we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about data processed by Google are best made to Google directly; we will help if you contact us.
  • Türkiye (KVKK, Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data): we do not process your personal data ourselves; the processing described in Section 2 is done by Google. You have the rights listed in Article 11 of the law, including to learn whether your data is processed, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK). Write to us and we will help you direct a request to Google.
  • California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): we do not sell personal information for money. The use of the advertising ID by Google for personalized ads may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under some state laws. You can opt out by turning off ad personalization on your device (Section 2.2), and, where shown, through the choices in the game's consent form. We do not discriminate against you for using these rights.
